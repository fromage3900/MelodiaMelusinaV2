# P3 Monolith Plan — Horizon Eater

**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Status:** preferred P3 direction; concept/mechanics lock, implementation soft-lock  
**Core thesis:** **distance may be anatomy.**  
**Creature fantasy:** a filter feeder whose feeding mouth is so large that, from the player's point of view, **the mouth becomes the horizon**.

---

## Why this should follow God That Molts

The opening Monolith sequence should escalate the kind of assumption the player is forced to abandon:

```text
P0 — Sea Above
water may be anatomy

P1 — Faraway Mother
fabric may be landscape / draped anatomy

P2 — God That Molts
geology may be discarded biology

P3 — Horizon Eater
distance / adjacency may be feeding anatomy
```

P3 is the first chapter where the category error stops being only **what the landscape is** and begins affecting **the relationship between places**.

It should feel visually spacious and beautiful after P2's dense ravines and layered shed-material ecology.

---

# Core creature concept

The Horizon Eater is not a giant mouth floating in the sky.

It is an impossibly large filter-feeding organism whose normal feeding strategy has become geography.

Real-world filter feeders provide the logic:
- baleen whales engulf large quantities of prey-bearing water and strain prey through baleen;
- rorquals expand throat grooves while engulfing;
- basking sharks ram-feed with the mouth held open;
- whale sharks can use suction-like bursts to pull concentrated food into the mouth.

Melodia translation:

> The Monolith does not swim through water to collect plankton. It changes adjacency so that distant matter, weather, pollen, animals, roads, and eventually whole landscapes pass through its feeding field.

Its visible mouth is **horizon-scale**.

The mountains/ridges the player has treated as distant scenery may actually be:
- jaw margins;
- filter plates;
- pleated throat anatomy;
- feeding lamellae;
- sensory ridges.

The organism feeds on diffuse ecological material over enormous areas. The player is not important prey.

That indifference is part of the horror.

---

# Region fantasy

A vast highland steppe / chalk grassland / wind-carved plateau.

Initial visual language:
- long pale grasses;
- violet/blue distance haze;
- chalk cliffs;
- isolated survey towers;
- ruined weather stations;
- migrating animals or birds;
- huge weather fronts;
- pollen / seed drift;
- distant white ridges and mountain teeth;
- expansive sky.

The level should initially feel unusually legible and peaceful.

The player can see major destinations from very far away.

That visual clarity becomes the trap.

---

# The first impossible rule

A distant landmark does not maintain a consistent distance.

Mara measures a white tower at roughly four kilometers.

After the party walks toward it for several minutes, she measures again.

It is farther away.

Later:
- A -> B and B -> A produce different repeatable distances.
- A tree can visually pass from behind a hill to in front of it without moving.
- two plant species from distant climates grow side by side;
- a bird crosses the horizon and briefly appears enormous because its geometric relation to the party changes;
- an echo returns from behind the singer;
- wind-driven particles bend toward a destination that is not visually nearby.

None of this should use glitch effects.

The world remains beautiful and physically calm.

---

# Biological feeding logic

The spatial anomalies are not arbitrary magic.

They are the Monolith's feeding mechanism.

## Feeding cycle

```text
1. Detect diffuse material / ecological density
2. Bend adjacency across a huge catchment area
3. Draw distant matter into shared flow corridors
4. Open horizon-scale gape
5. Pass gathered material through filter anatomy
6. Release / redistribute what is not retained
7. Adjacency relaxes
```

The player encounters steps 1–3 as “distance anomalies” before understanding steps 4–6.

## What counts as food

Do not define this too literally in P3.

Possible filtered material:
- pollen;
- plankton-like airborne organisms;
- mineral spores;
- seeds;
- atmospheric microorganisms;
- magical/biological particulate carried by Monolith ecosystems;
- perhaps resonance-bearing material rather than ordinary biomass.

This lets the Monolith be ecologically consequential without being a carnivore hunting people.

# P3 wardrobe: Glasswing Courier

Working ontology:

> **The world is air / continuity.**

Glasswing Courier should use:
- translucent wing-like cloth panels;
- weather-vane forms;
- insect-wing / survey membrane language;
- light aerodynamic silhouette;
- clear directional reads rather than dense ornament.

The outfit does not display a map.

It physically disagrees with visual distance.

Example:

```text
visual landmark: west
Mara's measurement: northwest
Glasswing panel tension: downward / toward a nearby fold
Sir Melodious response: behind the party
```

All observations can be valid.

# Primary P3 exploration verb — Wayfold

**Wayfold** is a temporary alignment where two places that appear far apart become locally adjacent.

This is not free teleportation and should not look like a portal.

The ideal experience:

> A cliff is hundreds of meters away. Melusina aligns Glasswing with a spatial fold, performs a short rhythm phrase, steps forward, and her foot lands on the far cliff as though the gap had briefly become one meter wide.

No vortex.
No loading tunnel.
No magical doorway.

The wrongness comes from continuity remaining visually intact.

# Party mechanics

## Melusina — interpret adjacency

She detects unstable spatial relationships and can temporarily align a Wayfold.

Tags:

```text
Explore.Clue.Distance
Explore.Clue.FilterFlow
Explore.Capability.Wayfold
```

Better rhythm:
- longer Wayfold stability;
- clearer destination alignment;
- optional side route;
- brief visibility of the larger feeding current.

## Mara — prove contradiction / Anchor

P3 is an important character chapter for Mara.

Her instruments should remain **correct**.

Example:

```text
A -> B = 4.2 km
B -> A = 760 m
```

Repeated tests return the same contradiction.

Anchor becomes literal spatial certainty:

> inside Mara's anchored radius, one meter temporarily remains one meter.

Uses:
- stop a Wayfold collapsing while the party crosses;
- stabilize a survey instrument;
- protect a small region from feeding-current compression;
- create a safe reference point during the gape sequence.

## Iris — prove matter crosses the fold

Iris finds material evidence that makes the spatial anomaly physical rather than optical:
- pollen from distant biomes;
- seeds from valleys not connected to the region;
- mineral dust from a mountain on another horizon;
- insects or spores whose ecological ranges should never overlap.

Her material analysis shows that the filter field transports real matter.

## Sir Melodious — impossible return

His resonance should become spatially unreliable in an authored way.

Strong beat:

> Sir Melodious sings a phrase. The answer returns before he finishes the last note.

Use once.

Do not turn the chapter into constant time paradoxes.

## Ebenezer — local sanity check

Mara places a brass survey tag near the party.

Ebenezer steals it, flies a short visible distance, and drops it.

Mara's instrument reports the tag several kilometers away while the party can still see him sitting beside it.

This gives a compact, funny, undeniable proof that the distortion is not just perspective.

# Experience structure

## Beat 0 — beautiful open country

Let P3 breathe.

The player receives a distant objective visible from the opening viewpoint.

No immediate horror.

## Beat 1 — inconsistent approach

Walking toward the objective does not reduce distance consistently.

Do not alter player speed secretly.

Change the world relationship, not locomotion feel.

## Beat 2 — ecological contradiction

Iris finds displaced pollen/seeds/material.

Mara records repeatable directional/distance inconsistency.

## Beat 3 — first Wayfold

Glasswing reveals a short safe fold between two obvious landmarks.

Teach:
- detect;
- align;
- rhythm phrase;
- step across;
- Mara Anchor optional/supportive.

## Beat 4 — filter-current clues

Atmospheric particles begin showing that many spatial folds point through the same enormous region.

Distant flocks and pollen streams all curve toward one horizon.

## Beat 5 — baleen geography

The party reaches formations previously read as:
- chalk ridges;
- stone fins;
- vertical erosion walls.

Closer inspection reveals repeated flexible/fibrous comb geometry.

Do not call it baleen in dialogue unless a character is making an analogy rather than identifying a species.

## Beat 6 — feeding onset

The world begins compressing into flow corridors.

Signs:
- distant weather fronts appear unnaturally adjacent;
- pollen and wildlife drift laterally across impossible distances;
- multiple horizons align;
- far landmarks “stack” behind one another.

The party is being swept into a feeding catchment, not targeted.

## Beat 7 — the mouth becomes the horizon

This is the core reveal.

The entire distant skyline changes interpretation.

What appeared to be two enormous mountain/ridge systems on opposite sides of the vista are the margins of a gape.

The dark atmospheric band between them is not storm shadow.

It is depth.

The horizon itself opens.

Do not show the whole head.

Suggested image:
- upper “skyline” begins to rise by an almost imperceptible amount;
- lower distant ridge sinks;
- vertical comb/plate silhouettes emerge in haze;
- cloud layers and migrating particles are drawn sideways through the opening;
- the field of view is too small to understand the whole mouth;
- party remains tiny and foreground-readable.

The player should realize:

> We have been walking inside the feeding radius of something whose mouth is larger than our visible world.

## Beat 8 — survive the filtration corridor

Not a boss fight.

Gameplay climax:
- chain authored Wayfolds;
- Mara anchors safe reference islands;
- Iris identifies material flow that will pass through filters rather than into retention zones;
- Sir Melodious reveals safe resonance returns;
- rhythm performance extends route clarity.

The party escapes because it understands the feeding mechanism, not because it injures the organism.

## Beat 9 — release

The mouth closes or passes beyond the region.

Distance slowly returns to ordinary behavior.

One landmark remains permanently displaced, proving the event physically rearranged the region.

# UE 5.8 implementation strategy

P3 should **not** attempt true non-Euclidean world geometry at engine scale.

The system should author the perception and traversal of impossible adjacency.

---

## 1. World Partition + HLOD

Use World Partition only when P3 is ready to become a genuinely large production region.

HLOD is essential for:
- persistent distant ridges;
- horizon-mouth anatomy;
- remote towers;
- broad landmarks that must remain visible while their source cells unload.

The Horizon Eater should mostly exist as:
- static distant anatomy;
- HLOD-compatible landmark assemblies;
- local foreground hero meshes;
- one controlled reveal animation/state change.

Do not build one huge skeletal mesh spanning the map.

---

## 2. Runtime Data Layers

Recommended P3 world states:

```text
DL_P3_NormalHorizon
DL_P3_WayfoldClues
DL_P3_FeedingAlignment
DL_P3_FilterGape
DL_P3_Aftermath
```

Data Layers can activate large authored geometry/atmosphere configurations at runtime.

Use them for **macro state**, while local MIDs/Niagara handle smooth transitions.

Do not toggle an entire Data Layer every time the player touches a Wayfold.

---

## 3. Wayfold implementation

Recommended first prototype:

```text
BP_WayfoldPair
  EntryTransform
  ExitTransform
  AlignmentSpline / direction cue
  DestinationPreviewData
  RequiredCapabilityTag
  StabilityDuration
  RhythmQualityMultiplier
  OptionalAnchorRequirement
```

### Crossing

When player crosses an authored threshold:
- preserve facing/camera relationship;
- transform the character from entry-local space into exit-local space;
- preserve local velocity where safe;
- move party companions through paired logic;
- keep camera motion continuous;
- use foreground occlusion/fog/terrain to hide unavoidable transform discontinuity.

The player should experience this as **one continuous step**.

No SceneCapture portal is required for the first prototype.

If a visible through-the-fold view later becomes essential, prototype that separately rather than making rendered portals a baseline dependency.

---

## 4. Streaming-safe Wayfolds

A long-distance Wayfold must not move the player into an unloaded destination.

Production sequence:

```text
Detect Wayfold
-> request/prewarm destination streaming
-> show alignment response
-> wait for destination readiness
-> permit crossing
-> teleport/transform player + party
-> release old streaming source after safety window
```

Use World Partition streaming sources when P3 reaches production-scale World Partition.

For a small prototype map, keep both locations in one loaded level and prove the mechanic first.

---

## 5. Filter-flow visualization

Use Niagara to visualize **matter flow**, not magic beams.

Best particles:
- pollen;
- grass seeds;
- dust;
- tiny insects;
- cloud wisps;
- loose textile remnants from prior regions only if narratively appropriate.

Flow direction should initially look like ordinary wind.

As feeding begins, many independent particle fields slowly agree on one impossible horizon direction.

Use Niagara Effect Types and aggressive distance/scalability policy.

---

Suggested parameters:

Sound progression:
- ordinary highland wind;

- filter plates produce huge soft fibrous/keratin-like movement rather than metallic teeth.

The creature should sound like weather being inhaled.

# Biological reference translation

Real biology is useful because it prevents the creature from becoming arbitrary cosmic magic.

Useful reference principles:

## Baleen whale engulfment

Rorquals can expand throat grooves to engulf prey-rich water, then filter retained prey through baleen.

Melodia translation:
- “mountain pleats” subtly expand during feeding;
- geography appears to gain capacity before the mouth becomes readable.

## Baleen / filter plates

Baleen is flexible keratinous filtering structure rather than teeth.

Melodia translation:
- horizon filter structures should be fibrous, comb-like, softly flexible, and weathered;
- avoid giant fangs.

## Ram feeding

Basking sharks can collect plankton by moving continuously with mouth open.

Melodia translation:
- the Horizon Eater may be migrating rather than opening because of the party;
- the party encounters the edge of a normal feeding pass.

## Suction / concentrated feeding

Whale sharks can rapidly expand the mouth and draw concentrated material inward.

Melodia translation:
- local Wayfolds become more severe as the feeding cycle intensifies;
- distant ecology is pulled into temporary adjacency before filtration.

The result should feel like an ordinary feeding strategy extrapolated until geography itself becomes fluid.

# Production guardrails

Do not:
- reveal a complete giant whale/shark body;
- make the mouth a literal skybox decal with teeth;
- use constant screen distortion/glitch effects;
- build true generalized non-Euclidean geometry;
- make every Wayfold a rendered portal;
- change player movement speed invisibly to fake distance;
- turn P3 into teleport-puzzle spam;
- make the Monolith intentionally hunt the party;
- require a map hundreds of kilometers wide.

Do:
- preserve recognizable landmarks;
- make contradictions repeatable;
- let Mara's measurements stay correct;
- show transported matter as physical proof;
- make Wayfold traversal quiet and tactile;
- sell the body through atmosphere, HLOD, silhouette, sound, and synchronized ecology;
- make the horizon-sized mouth the chapter's singular unforgettable visual.

---

# P3 prototype acceptance criteria

A contained P3 mechanics prototype succeeds when it proves:

1. one landmark can have two repeatable contradictory distances;
2. Glasswing visibly indicates a Wayfold without detective-vision UI;
3. a player can take one continuous-looking step between visibly distant ledges;
4. Mara can Anchor that relationship temporarily;
5. Iris can show physical matter transported through the fold;
6. Niagara particles establish a common filter-flow direction;
7. the skyline can transition from “mountains/weather” into **one horizon-spanning filter-feeder mouth** without showing the whole organism;
8. the gameplay climax is escape-by-understanding, not combat.

Player-summary target:

> “I thought the horizon was getting closer. Then the horizon opened its mouth.”
