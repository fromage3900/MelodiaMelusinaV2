# Poitou college lab: big cooks that earn their place in P0

Owner brief, October 22: use the school's additional RAM and full Houdini tools for ambitious experiments that produce reusable game assets. This is a proposed lab session, not a claim that these simulations or their Unreal integrations already work. Live evidence remains in `CURRENT_STATE.md`.

**Start from today's rebuilt `Tools/Poitou/hda/poitou_layout_live.hdalc` and its builder/VEX sources.** Preserve its canals, roads, roofs, foundations, lock and abbey. Do not substitute the old RoomShell Candidate or Oct-20 Unreal import. First earn one complete clay composition with joined roads/bridges, seated houses and visible water. Simulation detail cannot repair a disconnected town.

## The shortlist

| Priority | Experiment | Small pilot → ambitious cook | What comes home for P0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The singing lock: FLIP water released through the existing ecluse**, striking the rocky bank and splitting around the landing | Crop roughly 16 × 8 × 4 m; 24 frames at 0.16 m particle separation. Then 144 frames at 0.08 m. Try 0.04 m only after measuring the pilot, with narrow band enabled. Wedge three gate openings sequentially. | One hero mesh cache; velocity/flow, foam-source and wetness masks; a reusable collision-ready lock/river source. Keep Oceanology for broad water and use the cache for the local event. Their blend seam still needs a UE test. |
| 2 | **A real drainage and bank atlas**, anchored to the documented IGN Maillezais DEM | Protect village pads, route and canal waterlines with masks. Pilot a 250 m bank region at 1 m spacing; then the 1 km core at 0.5 m. Try one 100 m hero bank at 0.1–0.25 m, rather than eroding every square kilometre at that detail. | Height delta, sediment, debris, flow/flow-direction and wetness maps; bank/shore meshes; shared masks for existing landscape materials and PCG. No giant mountain erosion in the flat marsh. |
| 3 | **Maillezais ruin time machine: controlled masonry collapse** | One existing 2.4 m bay, 200–500 packed/proxy pieces, 48 frames. Then three connected bays, 2,000–5,000 pieces, 120 frames. Wedge intact / settled / breached states; keep the traversal route protected. | A small reusable ruin kit, grounded rubble clusters, stable collision proxies and optional short hero collapse. Bake settled states for normal exploration; do not run the whole abbey as runtime physics. |
| 4 | **Dawn breathing through the canals: sparse mist and musical foam** | Pilot a 20 × 10 × 8 m mist pocket, 48 frames at 0.25 m voxels. Refine to 0.125 m only if it adds readable detail. Render one mist puff and one water-impact sheet using Labs Flipbook Textures. | Reusable flipbooks, motion vectors where supported, bounds/pivot metadata and a restrained palette test for the existing Niagara/FluidNinja/material consumers. Loop quality and UE decoding are separate acceptance gates. |
| 5 | **An architectural seed orchestra**, useful after the clay layout works | Freeze the authored street/lot layout. Cook 12 then 48 variations of existing roof, opening, cornice, shutter and fence parameters through the shared kit. Reject intersecting footprints and blocked doors before high-detail cooks. | A measured preset library and contact sheet, not 48 competing town layouts. Three coherent families: canal house, village house and balais/barn; Melusina's fantasy house stays the singular exception. |

The FLIP proposal follows SideFX's [narrow-band workflow](https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/fluid/sopnarrowband.html); narrow band reduces deep interior particles but is not a fixed RAM guarantee. The erosion outputs and protected-mask approach follow [HeightField erosion](https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/heightfields/erosion) and [masking](https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/heightfields/masking.html). Packed/proxy destruction follows the [RBD workflow](https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/destruction/overview.html). Mist texture delivery uses [Labs Flipbook Textures](https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/nodes/out/labs--flipbook_textures-1.0.html).

## Run the school machine like a lab

1. **First 20 minutes:** record available RAM, CPU, GPU/VRAM, scratch capacity, Houdini build and actual license. Match 22.0.368 when possible. Open a copy, confirm units in metres and the latest graph's complete clay output; verify package paths before any overnight job.
2. **Pilot before spectacle:** measure wall time, peak process RAM, solver particle/voxel counts and cache bytes over 24–48 frames. Extrapolate duration and disk use, then add headroom. Halving 3D spacing can approach eight times the samples; 2D heightfields approach four times. Solver overhead and narrow bands change the actual result.
3. **Queue sequentially:** erosion/architecture first, then one FLIP hero wedge, then destruction or mist. Reserve at least 30% of physical RAM for the OS and other applications. Stop or reduce the job if memory keeps growing, paging begins, or the projected cache would consume the free scratch budget. More RAM does not establish GPU capacity.
4. **Cache deliberately:** keep raw `.bgeo.sc`/VDB sequences on approved local scratch, not the network drive. Separate simulation, meshing and render caches. Bring back the chosen deliverables, reconstructable source graphs, seed/settings, source hashes and pilot measurements. Bulk caches stay out of Git.
5. **End with proof:** one clay town overview, one player-height canal view, one short water/ruin preview, and a small exported pilot in UE. Check metre-to-centimetre scale, collisions, playback/decoding and the seam with Oceanology. A Houdini cook is not a playable-P0 proof.

## License and package check

The portable bundle stages Labs, qLib and zenv separately from the core town graph; individual cook compatibility must be checked. CityEngine/Palladio is optional: its published binary support has not established compatibility with Houdini 22, so it must not block tomorrow's work.

The home preview is limited-commercial `.hdalc`/`.hiplc`. A school Core/FX or Education seat is not a promise that these become unrestricted commercial files. Bring the raw Python/VEX builders and rebuild under the permitted school license; check any embedded child-asset licensing with the technician. Never rename extensions to imply conversion. SideFX documents [commercial conversion](https://www.sidefx.com/faq/question/convert-non-commercial-files-1/) as a separate process.

**Best long-term return:** finish one beautiful, reusable lock/river vignette and its mask library first. It improves the house garden, the Poitou canal route, later waterways and portfolio shots. The other experiments then expand a working world instead of obscuring unfinished geometry.
