## Light / colour notes
Swatches are pulled from midday/overcast daytime photos, not dawn: stops 1–2, 6 are dominated by duckweed/leaf greens and grey-green water; 3–5 by pale limestone creams, slate/terracotta roofs, sky blue; 7A/7B by warm limestone and (7A) snow-white. Dawn/mist grading must be authored — no openly licensed misty-dawn Marais photo was found in this pass.
