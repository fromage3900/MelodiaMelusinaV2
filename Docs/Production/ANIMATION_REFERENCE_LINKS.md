# Animation Reference Links

## 1. Body Mechanics Reference

Jumps, turns, lifts, falls, weight shifts, and physical actions.

### YouTube Playlists & Videos
- **[Motion Reference Clips for Animation](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLfea4ah7ujPK6wQ8uGVl46up8hKYeisSU)** — Slow-motion human motion reference for studying fundamentals of character movement.

- **[Lift Reference for Animators](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLfea4ah7ujPKKYzDy3vTw7fXPx4SV3Ne8)** — Real-life examples of people lifting objects; mechanics and nuance.
- **[Animation Body Mechanics - Roll, Push & Jump Reference](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLfea4ah7ujPKSOzN2ibr3HWez6rI1_tZt)** — Slow motion, real-time, and grid overlay options.
- **[Weight Shifting Animation | Body Mechanics Practice](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kCbnIyklK3U)** — Short clip on weight shifting and balance adjustment.
- **[Jump Forward Reference - Slow Motion + Grid](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s-GhHRS1PNo)** — Single jump reference with measurement grid.
- **[Animating Weight - Alexander Williams](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7VZljvWvF-Y)** — Tutorial on conveying weight in animation.
- **[Animating a Box Lift - Alexander Williams](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fEyOKsN-kiY)** — Character lifting a heavy object; anticipation and weight shift.
- **[Weight Lift Animation Exercise – nahkoten](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O4J-mpiMUzQ)** — Pre-lift stance, wide base of support, secondary movement.
- **[Lift Box Animation Reference – K A I J U](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=egDRS_6Ga8E)** — Anticipation, pause, stagger — excellent posing and weight shift.
- **[Weight Lift Animation – Philip Lol](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Lo6hlFSgck)** — Conveys difference between regular walk and carrying heavy weight.

### Websites
- **[Endless Reference](https://endlessreference.com/)** — Video membership app with slow-motion reference clips, grid overlays, PNG sequences at 24/30fps. Categories: Lift & Carry, Dance Moves, Jump Breakdown.
- **[Animation Mentor: Body Mechanics Breakdown](https://www.animationmentor.com/blog/animation-tips-tricks-what-makes-or-breaks-a-good-body-mechanics-shot)** — What makes or breaks a body mechanics shot; decision-making framework.

## 2. Acting Reference

Emotional performances, facial expressions, character acting.

### Film Scene References (Acting Breakdown Blog)
- **[Kyle Kenworthy's Acting Reference Blog](http://kylekenworthy.blogspot.com/p/acting-reference.html)** — Annotated film clips for animators: Mark Wahlberg subtle takes, Philip Seymour Hoffman hand poses, Tom Hanks eye work, Saoirse Ronan emotional range, secondary action examples from *The Town*, *Road to Perdition*, *It's Always Sunny*.

### Tutorials & Breakdowns
- **[Animation Mentor: How to Animate Facial Expressions and Emotion](https://www.animationmentor.com/blog/tutorial-how-to-animate-facial-expressions-and-emotion/)** — Myles Jackson breaks down 7 core facial expressions (happiness, sadness, anger, surprise, disgust, fear, contempt) with muscle anatomy.
- **[Animation Mentor: Animating Emotion Through Body Language](https://www.animationmentor.com/blog/tutorial-animating-emotion-through-body-language/)** — Natasha Krinsky on communicating emotion via posing, posture, movement (Inside Out breakdown).
- **[Animation Magazine: Using Video Reference for Natural Acting](https://www.animationmagazine.net/2023/03/toon-tips-using-video-reference-to-enhance-natural-acting-for-animation/)** — Chatrasal Singh's 5-step process: breathing, subtext, rhythm, shooting reference, importing to Maya.
- **[LAIKA Studios: Animating Kubo's Most Emotional Scenes](https://www.cgshort.com/animators-eye-animating-one-of-kubos-most-emotional-scenes-%e2%80%93-laika-studios_5237f02cb.html)** — Florian Perinelle on subtle emotion between Monkey and Beetle.
- **[Puss in Boots Panic Attack Scene Breakdown](https://www.thedailybeast.com/obsessed/how-puss-in-boots-pulled-off-that-incredible-panic-attack-scene/)** — Director Joel Crawford on collaboration, vulnerability, and authentic emotional performance in animation.

### Academic / Deep Dives
- **[Character Acting: A Case for Better Animation Reference](https://www.academia.edu/77223847/Character_Acting_A_Case_for_Better_Animation_Reference)** — Research paper on emotionally-driven vs. superficial acting reference.
- **[Facial Expressions for Empathic Communication of Emotion](https://journal.animationstudies.org/article/id/30/)** — FACS (Facial Action Coding System) applied to animation; Gollum case study.

### Tutorials & Videos

- **[An Introduction to Lip Sync (and Mouth Shapes) - Animation Theory](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h7U2YvmIMOo)** — Explaining mouth charts and phoneme categories.
- **[Phoneme Mouth Shapes for Lip Sync - Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com/ideas/phoneme-mouth-shapes-for-lip-sync/934879747388/)** — Curated collection of phoneme mouth shape references.

### Tutorials

- **[Walk Cycle Animation: Beginner's Guide](https://spotlightfx.com/blog/walk-cycle-animation-beginners-guide-to-creating-realistic-movement)** — Recording yourself, studying others, key poses.
- **[Walk Cycle - Dayna Keaney](https://blogs.ulster.ac.uk/daynke/2021/10/29/walk-cycle)** — Documenting a personality walk (tired, slumpy, spooky character).
- **[Week 7: ANI 260 - Key Poses and Mood](https://openlab.bmcc.cuny.edu/mmp260/week-7)** — Contact, passing, down, up poses; adjusting timing for emotion.
