### P0.2 — L_MelusinaMorning surfacing macro

**Goal:** directly support Environment Surfacing applications.

Pick a composition where several authored surface families meet:
- stone / rock
- foliage / grass
- architecture / ornamental trim
- ground dressing
- crystal / glitter / magical accents if they improve the composition

**Required output:**
- 1 beauty macro / medium shot

The companion may show:

- master → instance relationship

- shader parameter highlights

Do **not** turn the breakdown into a dashboard.

**DONE means:** a recruiter can see both finish quality and production reasoning in under 10 seconds.
