## 5. Choral Sheep material / fleece visual rule

The surface target is:

> **soft Nikki-quality wool first; hidden pearlescent cymatic embroidery revealed by song second.**

At REST:
- dusty / soft coat hierarchy;
- clean wool volume through normal, sheen, lighting and optional groom;
- readable face / silhouette;
- no noisy procedural albedo.

During Harmonize:
- pearl / lilac nodal line emergence;
- restrained iridescent sheen;
- slight emissive support;
- subtle local fleece lift / WPO only if it improves the form;
- beat pulse changes intensity, not pitch identity.

Avoid:
- rainbow audio visualizer treatment;
- full-body glow;
- high-frequency noise that destroys wool readability;
- patterning that erases the face or silhouette.
