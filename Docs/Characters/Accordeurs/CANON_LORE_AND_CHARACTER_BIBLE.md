- **UNKNOWN (U):** not established in provided source or Git; do not fabricate facts.

## World identity (E)
"Les Petits Accordeurs" are **Maintenance Spirits of Melody, Memory, and Small Repairs**. They care for Melodia's instruments, spaces, and memories; organize, map, deliver and preserve small things that hold the world in harmony. They work together despite individually specialized roles. They are found around instrument halls, libraries, ports, and places requiring care. About **15–25 cm** tall, with a sheet comparison of Melusina around 160 cm. Visual style: understated ink-and-watercolor storybook, pastel/neutral color spots, clean shapes, minimal ornament, soft textiles, lightly worn brass/wood/paper, expressive eyes. Never replace with the rejected hyper-detailed furry Atelier Magique look.

## Characters (E details, P interactions)
### ACC_COMMITTE — Monsieur Committe, Archivist
- **E:** lavender body; circular spectacles; forked leaf-like antennae; ledger, stamp, ink, seals. "Records what must not be forgotten."
- **P personality/voice:** meticulous, concerned about disappearing details; short scholarly chirps and careful ledger taps, not wordy exposition.
- **P animation:** shuffle to shelf, flip a page, stamp, inspect with magnifier.
- **P interaction:** recover missing inscriptions; reveal a preserved memory clue once narrative flag permits.
- **Visual don't:** turn ledger, glasses and stamp into a humanoid steampunk bureaucrat.

### ACC_MERGE — Mlle. Merge, Seamstress of Realities
- **E:** pale cream-pink body; soft side ears; loop/halo ornament; golden thread spool and needle; red-orange neck ribbon. "Mends what's frayed. Brings things together."
- **P personality/voice:** gentle, focused, quietly stubborn; humming, thread-tension clicks.
- **P animation:** draw luminous strand between two places, bow, tug, mend.
- **P interaction:** reconnect an authorized pair of broken musical nodes after player reveals their endpoints.
- **Visual don't:** erase the expressive soft ears, or interpret glowing thread as metallic weapon.

### ACC_CACHE — Petit Cache, Keeper of What Might Be
- **E:** sleepy blue body; oversized floppy navy sleeping cap; brown treasure satchel with star, shells, fragments. "Finds, keeps, and remembers the small things."
- **P personality/voice:** drowsy, protective of overlooked details; tiny yawns/chimes.
- **P animation:** nap in satchel, inspect fragment, present trinket.
- **P interaction:** retrieves a small misplaced token already approved by quest authority.
- **Visual don't:** inflate satchel into character body or replace cap with a realistic wizard hat.

### ACC_BUILD — Le Build, Foreman
- **E:** pale mint-green body; leaf ears; yellow hard hat with musical note; mallet/hammer; cheerful open-mouth expression. "Fixes, builds, and makes it work."
- **P personality/voice:** exuberant, persistent; wooden taps in rhythmic patterns.
- **P animation:** drag plank, tap twice, celebrate, balance heavy hammer.
- **P interaction:** cosmetic/physical repair of a simple bridge or resonance stand only after canonical progression signal.
- **Visual don't:** bulky workwear or heavily armored dwarf/robot proportions.

### ACC_LFS — Madame L.F.S., Quartermaster
- **E:** pale body; stern narrowed eyes; purple hair curl; oversized loaded backpack, keys. "Supplies what's needed. Keeps things in order."
- **P personality/voice:** unamused but caring; efficient key jingles.
- **P animation:** distribute supplies, count keys, sigh, organize mini helpers.
- **P interaction:** player-side supply handoff (data-authorized, no shadow inventory).
- **Visual don't:** modern realistic backpack straps/fur or excessive jewelry.

### ACC_REBASE — Monsieur Rebase, Cartographer
- **E:** mint body, long white beard, curling antenna; maps, scroll, pencil, compass. "Maps the seen and unseen. Finds the way."
- **P personality/voice:** patient and sometimes delightfully lost; parchment rustle, thoughtful murmurs.
- **P animation:** unfold large map, point route, get turned around then recover.
- **P interaction:** chart a previously unlocked musical route; never grants access without traversal flags.
- **Visual don't:** human elder anatomy or a full wizard costume.

### ACC_BOT — Le Bot, Errand-Runner
- **E:** lively coral-pink body; long heartlike antennae; mail satchel with letters, seals and tags. "Delivers, connects, and keeps things moving."
- **P personality/voice:** energetic and impatient; high chirps, rhythmic footsteps.
- **P animation:** sprint, distribute notes, hop and skid.
- **P interaction:** deliver queued quest letters and notifications through established narrative channels.
- **Visual don't:** oversized wings, realistic courier uniform, robot body.
