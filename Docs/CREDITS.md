# Melodia Project Attributions & Credits

The *Melodia* project is built on the generous work of the open-source, CC0, Fab /
Unreal Marketplace, and Epic sample communities — plus the owner's own first-party
content. Every asset below is credited to its individual creator with a direct
source link and its license, per our provenance policy. Local `PROVENANCE.md`
records (with SHA-256 checksums where applicable) live beside each staged pack
under `Imports/`, and `Docs/SOURCES_MATRIX.md` maps every `Content/` folder to its
credit row.

License key:

- **CC0 1.0 Universal** = public domain, no attribution required — we credit anyway out of respect.
- **BOOTH 0 JPY** = free download, per-creator terms (check the store page; typically free for commercial use with no attribution required, some request credit).
- **UE-Only (Epic sample)** = free Epic sample content, licensed for use only in Unreal Engine-based products, including commercial projects.
- **Fab / Marketplace EULA** = paid/free store content under Epic's standard marketplace license terms (per-product).
- **First-party** = original work by `fromage3900` (owner). No attribution required.
