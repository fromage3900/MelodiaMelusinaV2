# Toronto AI Startups — August 2026

Research snapshot for career targeting. Organized by relevance to this portfolio
(UE5 environment/tech art, procedural pipelines, AI tooling, game dev).

---

## Tier 1 — Direct fit (games + AI + UE/3D)

### Certain Affinity (Toronto office)
- **What:** AAA co-dev studio (Halo, CoD). Toronto + Austin.
- **Hiring:** Senior Advanced Technical Artist — UE5, materials/shaders,
  Houdini procedural, performance profiling. Reports to TA Director.
- **Salary:** $130K–$175K CAD
- **Fit:** Dead-center match. UE5 materials, procedural env art, Blueprint,
  Substance, Houdini. The portfolio's Substrate Toon spine and PCG work are
  directly applicable.
- **Link:** https://hiring.camp/job/yzlE9G

### Velan Studios (Toronto, hybrid)
- **What:** Innovative gameplay studio (Mario Kart Live, Knockout City). Custom
  engine + Unreal. Unannounced title.
- **Hiring:** Senior Environment Artist ($85K–$120K), Technical Artist
  Senior/Lead ($95K–$150K). Hybrid 3 days/week.
- **Fit:** Strong. Lighting, materials, NPR shaders, procedural art, Maya,
  Substance, ZBrush. The toon/NPR material work is relevant.
- **Link:** https://gamejobs.co/Technical-Artist-Senior-Lead-at-Velan-Studios-2062

### City From Naught (Toronto)
- **What:** AI-powered game studio. Text-to-3D asset generation. Titles:
  *Keyword 2: Nightfall* (open-world detective RPG, GDC 2024 Best in Play),
  *Keyword: A Spider's Thread*. Also ships *Marleen* (AI PBR texture tool).
- **Fit:** AI + game dev + 3D asset pipelines. Smaller studio, likely more
  generalist roles.
- **Link:** https://cityfromnaught.com/

### Databiomes (Toronto)
- **What:** On-device AI models for game chat/voice moderation. Ships *Ctrlvox*
  plugin on Epic Fab marketplace (UE integration). $1.2M CAD raised.
- **Fit:** UE plugin ecosystem, AI tooling for games. Technical but not art-focused.
- **Link:** https://betakit.com/databiomes-sets-sights-on-moderating-toxic-gaming-chats-with-first-ai-model/

### Chatforce (Toronto, U of T alumni)
- **What:** AI game creation platform — build/monetize games with autonomous AI
  agents. $2.1M pre-seed (July 2026).
- **Fit:** AI + game dev tooling. Early stage, likely seeking generalists.
- **Link:** https://app.fundz.net/fundings/chatforce-funding-round-seed-6946f1

## Tier 2 — Adjacent (AI infrastructure, not games)

### Cohere (Toronto HQ)
- **What:** Enterprise LLM lab. North platform, secure/private deployments.
  Partnered with U of T on responsible AI (July 2026).
- **Fit:** World-class AI company, but enterprise B2B focus. No games/3D.
  Relevant if pivoting toward AI infrastructure roles.
- **Link:** https://cohere.com/

### Waabi (Toronto)
- **What:** Physical AI — autonomous trucks and robotaxis. $1B+ raised
  (Jan 2026, largest Canadian tech round). Founded by Raquel Urtasun
  (Vector Institute co-founder).
- **Fit:** Simulation, 3D environments, synthetic data. Their *Waabi World*
  simulator could use environment art skills, but the domain is autonomous
  vehicles, not games.
- **Link:** https://waabi.ai/

### Uniflow Labs (Toronto, hybrid)
- **What:** Autonomous research intelligence platform. Seed stage. Hiring
  founding engineers (AI/ML, Platform/Rust, UX).
- **Fit:** AI tooling, but academic research domain. No 3D/games.
- **Link:** https://uniflow.tech/

### GPTZero (Toronto)

  for ML Engineer. 0.1%–0.3% equity.

- **Link:** https://wellfound.com/jobs/2716964-machine-learning-engineer

### Ada (Toronto)

### Publicus (Toronto)

## Market notes

- Toronto AI ecosystem is mature: Vector Institute, U of T ML programs,
  Cohere/Waabi as anchor companies.
- Game dev presence is smaller than Montreal but growing — Certain Affinity,
  Velan, 2K SportsLab are the established studios.
- AI-native game startups (City From Naught, Chatforce, Databiomes) are
  early-stage and small.
- Salary range for Senior Tech Artist: $95K–$175K CAD depending on studio
  size and seniority.
- Hybrid (2–3 days/week) is standard across both AI and game studios.
