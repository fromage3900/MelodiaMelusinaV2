## The paragraph (lead with the game)

Melodia Studio is a solo game-development entity building **Melodia Melusina**, a stylized
rhythm-JRPG in Unreal Engine 5.8. The game takes OMORI's intimate turn-based shape and adds a
musical layer that both sharpens combat and acts as a key in the world — timing modifies the
outcome of ordinary JRPG decisions, and played phrases open paths, the way Zelda's ocarina does.
Its visual and wardrobe bar is Infinity Nikki: a Substrate Toon material spine (916–1015
expressions across 12 parameter families) carrying six hand-authored fantasy fabric sets with
audio-reactive sheen and iridescence. What makes a project this size tractable for one person is
the pipeline underneath it — Blender 5.2 procedural geometry (49 Geometry Nodes builders across
30+ style genomes), a 77-graph PCG production library, and a typed MCP tool surface that lets
local open-weights models do real production work against contracts rather than prose. Every
claim the project makes about itself is backed by a gate ledger: nothing counts as done without a
row. The save/load, idempotency and packaged-launch gates are closed; the current work is
converging the rhythm, wardrobe, UI and world-puzzle systems into one integrated loop.

## What to keep honest when adapting this

- **The game is the product.** The pipeline is the credibility argument, never the ask.
- **Do not cite the MATH benchmark table.** Those figures were withdrawn 2026-08-19 and were
  never measured. Cite the gate ledger and the two real local-model probe results instead.
- **Blender is 5.2**, not 5.1. 5.1 is not installed.
- **"Playable" is a gated claim.** The four shipping gates (`runtime`, `save_load`,
  `repeat_consume`, `package_launch`) have PASS rows. The six orchestra convergence gates do not.
  Say which you mean.
