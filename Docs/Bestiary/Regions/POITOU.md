## Proposed design grammar

- Distinct from Faraway Mother: grasses, canal mud, limewashed stone, moss, wood, papery reed wings, river-glints rather than a competing blanket/velvet civilization.
- Prefer affectionate trickery, path misdirection, and small musical challenges over frightening aggression.
- No new dolls; preserve the completed moth and avoid duplicating its silhouette.
- No player-facing Bestiary UI requirement. A future approved creature may become a **Motif Creature** entry in the existing Compendium authority.

## Review gate

To promote any candidate: attach real reference provenance, one readable silhouette, an existing-scene role, an existing input / rhythm implementation path, a clear smallest build, and explicit owner sign-off. Until then, this is a research queue only.
