## Why the ignore was dangerous

This repository has already been one interrupted `git clean -fd` away from losing untracked content
(the 2026-08-08 incident that nearly erased the protagonist). A blanket ignore on a hand-authored
directory is the same hazard class: the files look present on disk and are absent from history.

- files above the 50 MB non-LFS threshold: **0**

## Why 92 of 104 files are tracked — and the other 12 deliberately are not

`git add melodia-design-system/` staged **92** files. The remaining 12 are two ignored directories,
and both exclusions are correct:

| Path | Rule that excludes it | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `social-cards/exports/` — 11 PNGs, 2.0 MB | `.gitignore:373` `Exports/` (matches case-insensitively on Windows) | **Correctly excluded.** These are generated by `social-cards/generate_social_cards.py`, which is tracked. Build output, not source. |
| `Saved/Logs/MonolithCalls.jsonl` | `.gitignore:5` `Saved/` | **Correctly excluded.** A tool log. |

Do **not** "fix" the `Exports/` rule to anchor it at `/Exports/` in order to capture the social cards.
That rule currently excludes 44 entries repo-wide, and anchoring it would silently un-ignore
`Content/Exports/`, `_TouchDesigner/exports/` and others — a much larger change than it appears.

If the social-card exports are ever wanted in history, the correct move is to commit them deliberately
with an explicit negation and a review of what else that negation releases.

## Reversibility

Re-adding `melodia-design-system/` to `.gitignore` would not delete tracked files, but it would stop
future changes from being recorded. Do not re-add it without a stated reason.
