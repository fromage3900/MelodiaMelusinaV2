# Board 01 — Creature + Fleece

## Production question

**What makes the Choral Sheep adorable and materially convincing before any technical effect is visible?**

## Reference targets

Collect/look for:
- real sheep and lamb face proportions;
- rounded forehead / muzzle silhouettes;
- ear and bell gesture;
- compact creature poses;
- wool clumps at macro scale;
- pearl, satin, organza, mohair, bouclé, and embroidered textiles;
- iridescence that appears in grazing light rather than constant neon;
- fiber breakup carried by normal/shear/sheens rather than painted albedo noise.

## Existing Melodia evidence

Use:
- `Docs/Handoffs/CHORAL_SHEEP_WOOL_LAB_REVIEW_2026-08-28.md`
- flat / Worley12 / lit findings;
- current conclusion: wool volume should come primarily from **form + normal + sheen + groom + lighting**.

## KEEP

- clean dusty coat values;
- large readable wool clumps;
- tiny highlight variation;
- face and ears remaining visually quiet;
- bell/trim as the strongest small accent;
- pearlescent response that emerges at grazing angles.

## AVOID

- dirty/mottled albedo used to fake wool;
- full-body equal-frequency glitter;
- rainbow coating;
- long fur obscuring facial expression;
- shader effects that make the sheep read as a VFX prop instead of a creature.

## APPLICATION

For Chladni Fleece, the resonance pattern should feel **woven under the wool**.

Think:
- embroidery under fleece;
- pearlescent seam;
- hidden textile structure;
- local clump lift along nodal lines.

The idle sheep must still be portfolio-worthy with Chladni intensity = 0.
