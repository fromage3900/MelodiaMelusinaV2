# Board 03 — Hyperpop / Rave Concert Dramaturgy

## Production question

**How can the capstone use the emotional force of a live electronic show without copying another artist's aesthetic?**

This board studies **composition and timing**, not branding.

---

## Ninajirachi — primary contemporary reference

### Coachella 2026 production / rehearsal diary
Source:
https://www.nylon.com/entertainment/ninajirachi-coachella-2026-photo-diary

What matters:
- production rehearsal is treated as a serious authored pass;
- large screen is a primary performer partner;
- high-energy DJ performance remains visually simple enough to read at distance;
- technological identity is part of the performance rather than hidden backstage.

**KEEP**
- screen-first composition;
- confident performer silhouette;
- production rehearsal as an explicit creative stage;
- giant graphic field behind a physically small performer.

**AVOID**
- copying her exact screen content, computer iconography, wardrobe, or album branding.

**MELODIA APPLICATION**
- tiny Choral Sheep against a gigantic harmonic field;
- Melusina/Sheep silhouette remains readable while TD visuals occupy architectural scale.

### Manchester live show — 2026
Source:
https://mancunion.com/2026/04/03/ninajirachi-live-gorilla/

Reported live detail:
- city-specific screen visuals;
- **MANCHESTER** displayed boldly behind the decks;
- the localized graphic itself became a crowd-response event.

**KEEP**
- typography/graphics used as an EVENT;
- local/contextual variation;
- one screen change creating a huge audience response.

**MELODIA APPLICATION**
Instead of city names, use context-specific musical identities:
- **C**
- **III**
- **V**
- `HARMONIZE`
- flock/pitch sigils;
- chapter-specific glyphs.

They should appear sparingly, at structural moments.

### Terminal 5 — 2026
Source:
https://www.papermag.com/ninajirachi-tour-photos

What matters:
- visible relationship between performer and crowd;
- audience participation becomes part of the musical image;
- high-energy electronic performance does not require complicated scenic construction.

**KEEP**
- call-and-response;
- gesture;
- audience/companion response;
- the performer causing the room to act.

**MELODIA APPLICATION**
Choral Sheep should visibly answer one another.
The response itself is part of the spectacle.

## Frost Children — bodily energy + imperfection

### Live hyperpop/electroclash energy
Sources:
https://dailynexus.com/2026-06-05/frost-children-bring-high-energy-hyperpop-to-the-hub/
https://www.ringtonemag.com/articles/frost-children-are-bigger-than-ever-but-their-shows-remain-charmingly-grounded

What matters:
- visual theatrics can coexist with messiness and physicality;
- performers/crowd behave like part of one event;
- movement and participation sell the energy as much as screen design;
- strong character-specific props/details can become memorable stage motifs.

**KEEP**
- sweat/physicality;
- imperfect movement;
- participation;
- one eccentric prop/detail;
- moments that feel authored but not sterile.

**MELODIA APPLICATION**
Do not make the sheep's Harmonize animation perfectly mechanical.
Let ears, bell, wool and tiny body motion preserve creature charm.

## A. G. Cook / PC Music — graphic identity + mythmaking

Sources:
https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/61554/1/a-g-cook-pc-music-takeover-dazed-2023-defined-generation-pop
https://www.dazeddigital.com/pc-music-takeover
https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/gallery/33024/4/timothy-luke-pc-music-takeover-2023

What matters:
- live identity can be deliberately larger-than-life;
- visual design, typography, product-like graphics and music can share one language;
- simple graphics can become mythology through repetition.

**KEEP**
- reusable visual grammar;
- recurring symbols;
- typography as worldbuilding;
- image system that extends beyond one concert.

**MELODIA APPLICATION**
Chladni Fleece should generate reusable Melodia visual motifs for:
- capstone;
- title cards;
- TD performance;
- website/editorial later;
- eventual in-world performance scenes.

## 2hollis — one impossible stage object + silhouette

Sources:
https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/music/story/2025-10-24/the-rise-of-2hollis-an-altadena-phoenix-star-shrine-auditorium
https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2025/11/6/2hollis-concert-review/
https://www.buzzgeneration.nyc/project-2hollis-at-coachella

What matters:
- one giant white tiger can dominate stage memory;
- strong white/red lighting and silhouette can carry entire sequences;
- haze creates depth and turns light into geometry;
- lighting design can use simple repeated forms to create complex patterns.

**KEEP**
- one huge memorable object/form;
- silhouette moments;
- haze/depth;
- light as architecture;
- simple geometry becoming complex through repetition.

**MELODIA APPLICATION**
Our equivalent is not a tiger.

It could be:
- one enormous Chladni ring;
- one impossible pearl membrane;
- one giant flock sigil;
- one water standing-wave wall.

Use **one**, not all four.

## Live-show rule extracted for Capstone 01

```text
QUIET
small creature / dark field
    ↓
ROOT HIT
one giant motif
    ↓
INTERVAL HIT
interference / split
    ↓
RESOLUTION HIT
field snaps into coherence
    ↓
WORLD HIT
Unreal environment physically answers
    ↓
AFTERIMAGE
return to intimate sheep
```

### Translation rule

**Steal the timing, not the branding.**

Do not import:
- logos;
- exact artist palettes;
- proprietary screen art;
- costumes;
- album iconography.

Translate the dramaturgy into Melodia's own:
- pearl;
- embroidery;
- water;
- chromatic pitch identity;
- resonant creatures;
- fantasy architecture.
