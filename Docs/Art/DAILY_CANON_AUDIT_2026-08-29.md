## Canon changes locked today

### 1. Companion-bird motif: cap it

Companion parrots/cockatoos are a **recurring world motif, not a party-wide mascot assignment system**.

Current ceiling:
- **2 strongly authored companion parrots** are canon-planned now;
- a **third bird may exist later** only if it fills a substantially different ecological or narrative role;
- do not give every party member a matching bird.

The world can contain many ordinary birds. The cap applies to named, party-associated companion parrots.

### 2. Sir Melodious remains singular

Sir Melodious remains the primary fully characterized avian companion:
- iridescent white cockatoo;
- genuine compact parrot/cockatoo anatomy;
- long treble-clef crest;
- deep green coat with plum lining and silver trim;
- dark glossy avian eyes with **no human-like sclera**;
- Resonance Call / distant environmental response identity.

He is allowed the most theatrical clothing and symbolic characterization of the companion birds.

### 3. Ebenezer is Mara Vell's companion

**Name:** Ebenezer  
**Species anchor:** green-cheek conure  
**Sex/pronouns:** male / he  
**Role:** Mara's field companion, scout, tactile anomaly tester, and unofficial survey colleague.

Preserve unmistakable green-cheek-conure identity:
- compact sturdy parrot body;
- dark head;
- bright pale eye ring;
- green back/wings;
- scalloped chest;
- warm red/orange belly and tail accents;
- curved conure beak;
- short-necked, tactile, climbing/perching body language.

Fantasy stylization may exaggerate:
- graphic feather groupings;
- richer patterned plumage;
- a few lyrical/ornamental feather shapes;
- asymmetrical field harness details;
- slightly elongated tail accents;
- expressive silhouette and pose language.

**Do not push him into falcon/hawk anatomy.** No raptor brow, long raptor legs, predatory chest silhouette, or eagle-like wing planform.

Ebenezer's gameplay/narrative distinction from Sir Melodious:
- Sir Melodious reads **resonance, song, pressure, and distant impossibility**;
- Ebenezer reads **immediate physical reality through touch, balance, curiosity, biting, pecking, and object interaction**.

Useful behavior: Ebenezer tests suspicious cloth, instruments, seams, ledges, and material changes before Mara is willing to call them evidence.

### 4. Mara Vell visual direction updated

Mara remains **Mara Vell**. Do not rename her to Electra.

A private collaborator photo reference supplied during ideation established a stronger visual lane:
- dark, dense, graphic curls;
- youthful but adult stylized face;
- expressive eyes without photoreal portrait rendering;
- practical, slightly oversized field layers;
- strong contrast between precise instruments and handmade/weathered clothing.

**Privacy rule:** source photos of real collaborators are local/private reference and should not be committed to the public repository without explicit permission.

The generated sheet that contained the labels `Electra Vell` / `Electra & Ebenezer` is **style exploration only**. Its embedded name is an image-generation artifact, not canon.

Target rendering language for Mara:
- same stylization family as the successful Melusina reference sheets;
- painterly/illustrative rather than rendered realism;
- simplified facial planes;
- slightly enlarged, readable eyes;
- graphic hair mass and silhouette;
- elongated but grounded fashion proportions;
- visible brush/ink/value grouping;
- avoid realistic skin rendering and fashion-photo anatomy.

### 5. Reverse: 1999 is an acknowledged influence, not a template

Reverse: 1999 is a meaningful inspiration for:
- archival/illustrated character presentation;
- eccentric historical-fashion silhouettes;
- literary props and instruments;
- melancholy restraint;
- ornamental document/UI language;
- character-specific material and cultural detail.

Melodia must translate that influence through its own core pillars:
- rhythm as world-reading;
- fashion as functional perception;
- impossible ecology;
- anatomy at landscape scale;
- Monoliths built from ordinary biological behavior at impossible scale;
- painterly sea-glass, cloth, organic, brass, pearl, and hand-authored material languages.

**Do not copy** Reverse: 1999's exact face construction, costume motifs, UI frames, symbols, or character archetypes.

### 6. P1 chapter changed: Faraway Mother

The Reef That Looks Back is **no longer P1**.

Current chapter order:

```text
P0 — Sea Above
    water may be anatomy

P1 — Faraway Mother
    fabric may be landscape / geography may be draped anatomy
    introduce Mara Vell + Ebenezer

P2 — The God That Molts
    geology may be discarded biology
    preferred introduction point for Iris Fen
```

This change is deliberate: two consecutive water-dominant chapters would falsely teach the player that Melodia is primarily about strange oceans rather than category errors across all of nature.

### 7. Reef That Looks Back is deferred, not discarded

The Reef That Looks Back / Glassgarden Shoals remains a strong later Monolith candidate.

Keep its best ideas for a later return to water:
- landscape surface as chromatophore skin;
- reef copying party palettes/silhouettes;
- small wound revealing living tissue;
- kilometer-scale eye-region reveal;
- Starskiff Current Rail climax.

The existing P1 plan should no longer be executed as chapter one after Sea Above.

### 8. Starskiff work remains valid but is decoupled from P1

All Starskiff/Oceanology architecture work remains useful:
- `BP_Starskiff`
- `BPC_StarskiffMovement`
- `BPC_StarskiffOceanologyAdapter`
- `BP_StarskiffRail`
- dedicated Starskiff input context
- Skim / Current Rail / Anchor Brake / Sounding Leap concepts.

However, **Faraway Mother does not require the Starskiff**.

The Starskiff can be prototyped independently as post-P0 traversal R&D and brought back when a water/current chapter genuinely needs it.

### 9. P2 preferred direction: The God That Molts

P2 should broaden the Monolith vocabulary again.

Core thesis:

> What looked like bark, cliff, mineral sheet, ruin, or geological strata is discarded biological material from something still alive elsewhere.

Preferred new party character: **Iris Fen**, currently a proposed/soft-locked apothecary-materialist rather than a fully final character.

Her likely design/gameplay lane:
- ecology / chemistry / residues / dyes / reagents;
- reveal material state rather than generic elemental magic;
- proposed verb: **Catalyze** — accelerate, interrupt, expose, crystallize, soften, or reveal material reactions.

Do not over-lock Iris's final costume, instrument, or backstory until P1's character workload is understood.

### 10. Custom player character is not current scope

A full customizable player/avatar is **not part of P0–P2 scope**.

Safe customization lane:
- authored Melusina remains protagonist;
- outfits;
- dyes;
- trims;
- charms;
- hats/accessories;
- later, a small number of authored hairstyle/cosmetic variants if production allows.

The generated `Player 2` / customizable-character concept was exploratory image-model drift, not a feature commitment.

### 11. Calypso is non-canon

`Calypso — Keeper of Shorewake` was invented by an image-generation pass and was never approved as a party member.

Status: **NON-CANON / parked visual fragment**.

If useful later, individual visual ideas may be recycled into an NPC, artisan, shrine keeper, or regional costume. Do not treat Calypso as a current roster character.
