# Mara Vell + Ebenezer — Stylization Target

**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Status:** art-direction lock for future corrected concept sheets  
**Purpose:** preserve the successful stylization lane without publishing private collaborator source photos or treating generated text errors as canon.

---

## Core target

Mara and Ebenezer should live in the same illustrative world as the successful Melusina reference sheets:

> **designed game characters first, believable anatomy second, painterly realism last.**

The failed passes were too close to rendered portrait/concept realism. The successful pass worked because it simplified and graphicized the reference rather than merely repainting it.

---

# Mara Vell

## Face / proportion language

Target:
- adult young-woman proportions;
- slightly enlarged readable eyes, but never chibi;
- simplified nose/mouth planes;
- painterly cheek/value shapes rather than pore/skin realism;
- graphic eyebrow and lash shapes;
- face should read immediately at character-select / dialogue-portrait scale;
- subtle asymmetry and expressive posture.

Avoid:
- photo-derived facial rendering;
- realistic skin microdetail;
- fashion-editorial cheekbone exaggeration;
- generic semi-realistic fantasy heroine face;
- exact replication of a private collaborator's likeness.

## Hair

Preferred silhouette:
- dense dark curls;
- large readable curl masses before individual strands;
- irregular flyaways and asymmetry;
- curls may hold small practical survey clips, pins, or one restrained ornament;
- silhouette should remain recognizable in flat black.

Do not solve the hair as thousands of equal realistic curls.

## Costume

Design equation:

```text
field cartographer
+ naturalist
+ practical Edwardian workwear
+ hand-authored Melodia asymmetry
- generic steampunk
```

Primary shapes:
- layered survey coat / mantle / vest rather than one clean uniform;
- slightly oversized sleeves or outer layer;
- compact fitted inner structure;
- belt/satchel/tool cluster concentrated around one side;
- sturdy boots;
- readable cloth panels that can move in Faraway Mother wind/tension scenes.

Palette:
- indigo / midnight violet;
- worn brown / umber;
- parchment cream;
- oxidized teal / sea-glass accent;
- warm brass;
- tiny coral/rust note.

Material hierarchy:
1. worn woven cloth;
2. aged leather;
3. brass/copper instrument hardware;
4. sea-glass/resonance glass as a small hero accent.

## Instrument/tool presentation

Mara should look like someone who actually uses her equipment.

Preferred hero props:
- Sounding Staff;
- English concertina;
- compact survey compass / resonance gauge;
- field journal;
- map tube / cloth map rolls;
- Meridian Heart details mostly hidden.

Avoid decorating every belt slot with unrelated gadgets.

# Ebenezer

## Species anchor

Ebenezer is a fantasy-stylized **green-cheek conure**, not a generic bird of prey.

Preserve:
- compact round/sturdy torso;
- short neck;
- curved parrot beak;
- pale eye ring;
- dark head;
- green wing/back mass;
- scalloped chest;
- red/orange belly;
- warm red tail;
- zygodactyl parrot feet and climbing behavior.

## Fantasy push

Allowed exaggeration:
- graphic scalloped feather plates;
- irregular teal/indigo edges in flight feathers;
- coral/gold accent transitions;
- slightly longer elegant tail tips;
- two or three lyrical stray feather shapes;
- small asymmetrical harness/field charm;
- stylized eye shape and posture.

Not allowed:
- falcon brow;
- eagle chest;
- hooked raptor silhouette replacing parrot beak;
- long predatory legs;
- pointed hawk wing planform;
- armored battle-bird treatment.

The fantasy identity should come from **pattern, ornament, expression, and behavior**, not changing species anatomy.

## Gear

Keep gear minimal:
- lightweight survey harness;
- engraved ID/leg band;
- one resonance charm or tiny retrieval loop;
- optional small tag pouch only if it animates cleanly.

Sir Melodious owns the theatrical wardrobe lane. Ebenezer should look like a working field companion.

# Relationship posing

Mara + Ebenezer should be designed as a duo, not two isolated model sheets.

Required pose studies for the next corrected sheet:
- Ebenezer perched on Mara's forearm while she writes;
- Ebenezer leaning into / biting a cloth sample while Mara measures it;
- Ebenezer perched on Sounding Staff or survey instrument;
- Mara trying to retrieve a stolen calibration tag;
- quiet shoulder/perch pose showing trust;
- Faraway Mother signature beat: Ebenezer tugs local cloth and both react to the distant fold twitch.

Their comedy should come from established working familiarity, not slapstick pet ownership.

# Rendering language

Use:
- cream/parchment negative space;
- thin imperfect ink contour;
- watercolor/gouache value grouping;
- selective sharp details on eyes, instrument faces, embroidery, and beak;
- broad soft treatment elsewhere;
- flattened illustrative lighting rather than cinematic realistic key/fill rendering;
- ornamental annotation language inspired by archival field documents.

Target proximity:

```text
Melusina successful sheets
    ↓ same visual family
Mara / Ebenezer corrected sheet

NOT
photo reference → realistic fantasy painting
```
