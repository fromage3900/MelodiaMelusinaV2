# Mara Elettra Vell

## Face / proportion language

Target:
- adult young-woman proportions;
- slightly enlarged readable eyes, but never chibi;
- simplified nose/mouth planes;
- painterly cheek/value shapes rather than pore/skin realism;
- graphic eyebrow and lash shapes;
- face should read immediately at character-select / dialogue-portrait scale;
- subtle asymmetry and expressive posture.

Avoid:
- photo-derived facial rendering as the final target;
- realistic skin microdetail;
- fashion-editorial cheekbone exaggeration;
- generic semi-realistic fantasy heroine face;
- losing the graphic curl/eye/silhouette language established by the primary sheet.

## Hair

Preferred silhouette:
- dense dark curls;
- large readable curl masses before individual strands;
- irregular flyaways and asymmetry;
- curls may hold small practical survey clips, pins, lenses, or restrained ornaments;
- silhouette should remain recognizable in flat black.

Do not solve the hair as thousands of equal realistic curls.

For 3D, prioritize the silhouette from the primary sheet before strand realism.

# Ebenezer

## Species anchor

Ebenezer is a fantasy-stylized **green-cheek conure**, not a generic bird of prey and not a sun conure.

Preserve:
- compact round/sturdy torso;
- short neck;
- curved conure/parrot beak;
- pale eye ring / facial contrast;
- dark head;
- green/teal wing and back mass;
- scalloped chest;
- red/orange belly;
- warm red tail;
- zygodactyl parrot feet and climbing behavior.

The primary sheet pushes his plumage into a jewel-like fantasy palette. This is canonical stylization as long as the underlying green-cheek-conure body plan remains legible.

## Fantasy push

Allowed exaggeration:
- graphic scalloped feather plates;
- irregular teal/indigo edges in flight feathers;
- coral/gold accent transitions;
- slightly longer elegant tail tips;
- two or three lyrical stray/antenna-like feather shapes;
- small asymmetrical harness/field charm;
- stylized eye shape and posture.

Not allowed:
- falcon brow;
- eagle chest;
- hooked raptor silhouette replacing parrot anatomy;
- long predatory legs;
- pointed hawk wing planform;
- armored battle-bird treatment;
- sun-conure yellow/orange body replacing the dark-headed green-cheek base.

The fantasy identity should come from **pattern, ornament, expression, and behavior**, not changing species anatomy.

## Gear

Keep gear minimal:
- lightweight survey harness;
- engraved ID/leg band;
- one resonance charm or tiny retrieval loop;
- optional small tag pouch only if it animates cleanly.

Sir Melodious owns the theatrical wardrobe lane. Ebenezer should look like a working field companion.

# Relationship posing

Mara + Ebenezer should be designed as a duo, not two isolated model sheets.

The primary sheet's relationship panel is direct canonical pose/behavior reference.

Required production pose studies:
- Ebenezer perched on Mara's forearm while she writes;
- Ebenezer leaning into / biting a cloth sample while Mara measures it;
- Ebenezer perched on Sounding Staff or survey instrument;
- Mara trying to retrieve a stolen calibration tag;

Their comedy should come from established working familiarity, not slapstick pet ownership.
