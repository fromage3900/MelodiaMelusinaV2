# Monolith Concept Art Backlog — 2026-08-26

Purpose: track which abstract overworld Monolith concepts already have generated visual reference, which still need standalone renders/design sheets, and how to stage the remaining art work in narrow batches.

This is a planning-only art/design document. It does **not** add or replace Unreal assets, textures, LFS binaries, gameplay code, or generated image files.

## Visual direction

These Monoliths should read less like oversized enemies and more like serene, impossible environmental events with partial anatomy. The production rule is:

> Model only the anatomy the player must understand; imply the rest with terrain silhouette, water volumes, fog, lighting, Niagara/VFX, shaders, scale cues, and environmental aftermath.

Target feeling: magical serenity escalating toward ontological/existential terror, with water, rhythm, fashion/semiotics, and AAA environment composition at the center.

## Render/design-sheet plan by concept

### P0-01 — The Sea Above

Core phenomenon: a second ocean appears upside-down beneath the real ocean; up/down and sky/sea begin collapsing.

Concealed form: inverted jellyfish or siphonophore suspended below the seabed, with translucent bell mass mistaken for a mirrored world.

Required images:

1. **Distant sighting** — boats crossing a calm sea while a second upside-down horizon glows below the surface.
2. **Mid reveal** — cliffside view showing the translucent bell mass pulsing under/through the sea, with villages as scale cues.
3. **Traversal close-up** — player moving along inverted-water membranes, dangling tendrils, floating debris, and gravity-confused rain.
4. **Production sheet** — labeled bell mass, tendril curtains, false-sky membrane, playable surface strips, VFX-only regions.

Implementation notes:

- Model: 1 translucent bell shell, 3–5 tendril ribbon variants, traversal membrane strips.
- Imply: full body via volumetric fog, inverted reflections, water shader planes, cloud/debris parallax.
- VFX: upward rain, fish-air silhouettes, refractive distortion, underwater skybox swap.
- Gameplay: rhythm stabilizes local gravity; fashion silhouettes with celestial/marine motifs affect whether membranes open as paths.

Concept prompt:

> A serene dark-fantasy ocean at twilight where the sea surface reveals a second upside-down ocean and sky beneath it, an impossible translucent jellyfish/siphonophore bell larger than a bay pulsing under the world, tiny boats and cliffside ruins for scale, gravity confused, rain falling upward, magical blue-violet refraction, AAA cinematic environment concept art, labeled production-sheet companion views.

### P0-02 — The Last Reflection

Core phenomenon: every reflective surface begins showing the same empty ocean, regardless of location.

Concealed form: colossal ray/flatfish living inside reflection space, enlarging its habitat by making the world more mirror-like.

Required images:

1. **Distant omen** — tide pools, mirrors, polished stone, and lakes all showing the same pale ocean.
2. **Creature glimpse** — manta/ray silhouette moving beneath a puddle that appears deeper than the real world.
3. **Mirror breach** — reflective water expanding over architecture, swallowing a shoreline shrine.
4. **Production sheet** — labeled reflection portals, ray silhouette, material states, playable/no-playable reflective zones.

Implementation notes:

- Model: simplified ray silhouette, fin/tail landmarks, reflective portal frames.
- Imply: body through screen-space/reflection shaders, planar reflections, fogged mirror-ocean skybox.
- VFX: reflection lag, duplicated horizon, ripple portals, mask-based material takeover.
- Gameplay: reflective clothing lets the entity perceive/track the player; rhythm desynchronizes or aligns mirrored terrain.

Concept prompt:

> Moonlit basalt coast filled with tide pools and broken mirrors, every reflection showing the same impossible pale ocean, a vast manta-ray silhouette gliding beneath a puddle as if the puddle contains another world, gothic coastal monolith, tiny lone figure for scale, cold silver-blue lighting, serene existential dread, AAA concept art plus labeled production design sheet.

### P1-01 — The Unfinished Whale

Core phenomenon: different parts of the same whale appear hundreds of kilometers apart, and the world tries to complete the missing anatomy.

Concealed form: whale discontinuously distributed through space; geography becomes ribs, veins, organs, and skin folds.

Required images:

1. Tail near one coast, head implied beyond mountains.
2. Valley/rib landscape where roads and rivers begin forming whale anatomy.
3. Map-like anatomical overlay: geography-to-body correspondences.
4. Production sheet: head/tail landmarks, rib-valley kit, organ-cloud forms, stable playable surfaces.

Implementation notes:

- Model only head/tail/rib fragments and traversal surfaces.
- Use terrain dressing, spline rivers, cloud/volumetric organ forms, and minimap/world-map foreshadowing.
- Gameplay: player prevents reality from deciding the world is part of the animal.

### P1-02 — The Crownless Stag

Core phenomenon: all forests begin migrating toward invisible antlers.

Concealed form: colossal underground stag whose antlers emerge hundreds of kilometers apart; the forest becomes its crown/silhouette.

Required images:

1. Forests leaning/crawling toward two impossible dead-tree antlers in the clouds.
2. Aerial silhouette where the biome itself forms a stag.
3. Close-up of roots, bark, and living-antler interfaces.
4. Production sheet: antler landmarks, moving forest VFX, root traversal, ecosystem stakes.

Implementation notes:

- Model antler spires, root bridges, bark/stone close-up kits.
- Imply full body with biome-scale tree orientation, wind, fog, and map silhouette.
- Avoid making it feel like a normal deer boss; the readable body is the forest pattern.

### P1-03 — The God That Molts

Core phenomenon: exponentially larger shells are discovered across the world; the actual organism may now be too large to perceive.

Concealed form: cicada/crustacean/aquatic arthropod god leaving shells the size of towns, mountains, then regions.

Required images:

1. Settlement built inside an old hollow shell.
2. Fresh larger shell cracking open across a mountain range.
3. Scale-sequence design sheet of shells 1–4.
4. Production sheet: shell material, interior traversal, fracture/VFX, stable-reality islands.

Implementation notes:

- Model reusable shell kits at multiple scales, fracture seams, translucent old chitin materials.
- Imply current organism with off-screen footsteps, gravity shifts, stars vanishing, shell aftermath.

## Definition of done for each concept-art sheet

A concept is considered visually covered only when it has at least one standalone sheet or cinematic render that includes:

- clear concept title;
- core phenomenon;
- visible or implied creature form;
- one distant overworld scale cue;
- one close material/anatomy detail;
- modeled-vs-implied production notes;
- at least one gameplay/rhythm/fashion interaction hook.

Poster panels count as mood coverage, not full production coverage.
