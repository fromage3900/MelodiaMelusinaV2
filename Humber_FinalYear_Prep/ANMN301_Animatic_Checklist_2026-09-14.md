## What They Grade
Polished animatic from YOUR short film storyboard, via Blender VSE:
1. Full set of panels, labeled + numbered visually (no gaps)
2. Logical narrative order, consistent aspect ratio/resolution
3. Pacing per panel type:
   - Dialogue/acting 3-6s | Action 1-2s | Establishing 3-5s | Reaction 2-4s | Quick transitions 1-2s
4. Up to 3 simple dissolves/fades ONLY for clarity, not style
5. Title card at start + End card at close
6. Smooth playback, no broken frames, revised rhythm
