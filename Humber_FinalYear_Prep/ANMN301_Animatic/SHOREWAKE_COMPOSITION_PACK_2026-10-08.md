# SHOREWAKE — COMPOSITION PACK (2026-10-08)

Composition language for this film: a sleeping giant with small warm lights. The hull's
diagonal is the leading line (bow lower-left toward stern upper-right); the mast is the
vertical counter; the lantern glow is the focal anchor; pearls and lights are the sparkle
path; water is negative space. Rule learned from the tests below: **keep the boat's
iconic silhouette whole** — mast top, crow's nest, and bowsprit all visible.

## House rules distilled

1. Whole silhouette or a deliberately tight detail — nothing accidental.
2. The mast at a third, never dead-center; hull diagonal crossing the frame.
3. Leave the upper-right negative space for the sky story (stars, glow).
4. Waterline drama belongs at the waterline — keep the contact in frame for detail shots.
5. When a blind framing fails twice, stop — this is what the live session is for.
