# Humber AN311 Final Year — Weekly Routine (Sem 5/6)

> Capstone is king. Portfolio is queen. Everything else serves them.

## Time Blocks (suggested 45-50h week)

| Day | Morning (9-12) | Afternoon (1-5) | Evening (7-9) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Life Drawing 5/6 + review | 3D Art 5 lab / asset build | Capstone — blocking / animation |
| Tue | Animation Art 5 | Capstone — production | Portfolio — polish one reel shot |
| Wed | Animation History / GNED | 3D Art 5 + Post | Career Connections tasks (resume, ArtStation) |
| Thu | Life Drawing | Capstone — team sync / dailies | Render / lighting tests |
| Fri | Capstone — full production | Portfolio — edit reel cut | Light review / plan next week |
| Sat | Deep Work: Capstone (4h) | Reel render + feedback | OFF |
| Sun | OFF / life drawing practice | Weekly review (30m) | Prep Monday |

## Weekly Rituals (30 min each)
- **Monday 9am:** Pick 3 MUSTS for the week (e.g., "finish walk cycle", "model hero prop", "cut reel v0.3")
- **Friday 5pm:** Dailies — screen cap 3 shots, note what to fix
- **Sunday 7pm:** Backup check — external drive + cloud, naming audit

## Energy Tips
- Render overnight, not during work hours
- Batch feedback: collect notes, fix in one pass
- Keep life drawing sketchbook with you — 15m daily beats 3h cram
