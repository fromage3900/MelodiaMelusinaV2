♫ the journey

Authored to end. Built to keep going.

Melodia is not a seasonal treadmill and it is not an infinite system pretending to be a story. Each Volume is meant to have a real emotional ending. The architecture underneath it is simply designed so another Reverie, Chapter, Voyage, creature, outfit, or impossible horizon can be added without rebuilding the journey that came before it.

“The beautiful things aren’t rewards for playing the game. They are how you play the game.”

♬ little laboratories

Touch the ideas before they become machinery.

The browser experiments are sketches, not replacement engines. They exist so musical puzzles, cozy interface objects, visual language, chapter presentation, and world-schema ideas can become tangible in minutes. Unreal remains the runtime authority.
