and played phrases unlock the world. Shape: OMORI. Music-as-key: Zelda. Wardrobe bar: Infinity
Nikki. Architecture detail: [`SYSTEM_MAP.md`](SYSTEM_MAP.md), [`DATA_FLOW.md`](DATA_FLOW.md).
