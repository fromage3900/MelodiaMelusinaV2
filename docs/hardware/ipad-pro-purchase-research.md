# iPad Pro + Stylus Purchase Research

_Last updated: 2026-08-30_

## Goal

Create a durable buying reference for upgrading the current 2020 iPad Pro into a stronger portable ZBrush/concept-art workstation while keeping costs under control as a Humber College student in Ontario, Canada.

## Current working recommendation

For serious ZBrush for iPad use, the safest stylus path is still a genuine Apple Pencil because third-party pressure-sensitive pens generally depend on app-specific SDK support. The cheapest reliable route for the current 2020 iPad Pro is therefore a used/open-box/refurbished Apple Pencil 2 rather than a new one.

For the new iPad, the 13-inch iPad Pro remains the preferred form factor because the larger drawing/sculpting area is meaningful for ZBrush and concept work. The main configuration choice should be driven by memory needs rather than storage alone: 256 GB is the value option when the desktop remains the primary Unreal workstation; 1 TB is the more serious long-term ZBrush option when the higher-memory configuration materially improves dense sculpting headroom. Avoid paying extra for 512 GB if the goal is performance rather than storage.

## Stylus research for the current 2020 iPad Pro

### 1. Used / open-box Apple Pencil 2 — best practical buy

- Native pressure and tilt support.
- Magnetic pairing/charging on compatible 2020 iPad Pro models.
- Safest choice for ZBrush for iPad.
- Deep-research pricing found Canadian used/open-box/refurbished examples around roughly CAD $119–129, with marketplace pricing varying.
- Search Best Buy Canada open-box, Walmart Canada marketplace/open-box, eBay Canada, local used listings, and reputable refurb sellers.
- Watch for counterfeit Apple Pencils on marketplaces; prioritize sellers with strong return policies and serial/condition transparency.

### 2. SonarPen 2 — cheapest real-pressure experiment, but not recommended for ZBrush

- About US$45 direct (~CAD $60 before tax/shipping/conversion).
- Real pressure input and compatible with 2018+ iPads.
- Wired / USB-C and battery-free.
- Pressure depends on the app supporting SonarPen's SDK.
- No confirmed ZBrush for iPad support found in research.
- Treat only as an experiment for supported art apps, not as a dependable ZBrush replacement.

Official product page:
https://www.sonarpen.com/product/sonarpen2

### 3. Adonit Note+ / Note+ 2 — real pressure, app-dependent

- Adonit Note+: 2,048 pressure levels.
- Compatible with 2020-era 11-inch and 12.9-inch iPad Pro generations.
- Pressure is app-dependent rather than system-wide.
- No confirmed ZBrush for iPad pressure support found.
- Note+ 2 has similar pressure claims but some user reports mention pairing/pressure issues.
- Better thought of as an art-app-specific alternative than a ZBrush solution.

Official product pages:
https://www.adonit.net/products/note-plus
https://www.adonit.net/products/note-plus-2

## Cheap non-pressure backup stylus options

If pressure sensitivity is not required for the backup pen, the market becomes much cheaper and more colourful.

### ESR Geo Digital Pencil

- Good backup candidate.
- Commonly available in pink, purple, blue, black, and white depending on seller/stock.
- Palm rejection + tilt.
- Some versions include Find My support.
- No real pressure sensitivity.

### Regular ESR Digital Pencil

- Usually cheaper than the Geo version.
- Often available in pink/white/dark colours.
- Palm rejection, tilt, magnetic attachment, replaceable tips.
- No real pressure sensitivity.

### Metapen / JAMJAKE class of budget pencils

- Common Reddit-style budget recommendation for writing and general iPad use.
- Often inexpensive and available in cute colours.
- Usually tilt + palm rejection only.
- Do not confuse tilt sensitivity with actual pressure sensitivity.

## New 13-inch iPad Pro buying strategy

### Apple Education Store

Because this purchase is being made as a current Humber College student, always check Apple Education pricing before buying retail.

Current research found:
- 13-inch iPad Pro education pricing starts below regular retail.
- Apple Pencil Pro also has education pricing.
- Apple Canada's 2026 student promotion runs during the back-to-school period and may include an Apple Gift Card with eligible purchases.

Important: live Apple pricing has shown conflicting crawled values during research, so re-check the official Canadian Education Store on purchase day rather than relying on an old screenshot or note.

### Best Buy Canada — things to check before purchase

Before buying, compare all of the following:

1. Student / education promotions active on Best Buy Canada.
2. Open-box units sold directly by Best Buy.
3. Geek Squad Certified Open Box where available.
4. Trade-in value for the 2020 iPad Pro.
5. Price-match eligibility against an identical Canadian retail configuration.
6. Gift-card or bundle promotions.
7. Financing terms and whether they are actually better than Apple's current student pricing.
8. Whether any promotion excludes Apple products or cannot stack with open-box/price matching.

Do not assume student discounts stack with Apple products; verify the exact offer terms on checkout day.

### Other Canadian channels worth checking

## Trade-in checklist for current 2020 iPad Pro

Before requesting a firm quote, identify:

- 11-inch 2nd gen vs 12.9-inch 4th gen
- storage capacity
- Wi-Fi vs Wi-Fi + Cellular
- screen/body condition
- battery/charging status
- whether Find My / Activation Lock can be removed

Use trade-in value as a comparison tool rather than assuming it is the best net return. Private resale can be higher, but trade-in is simpler and lower-risk.

## Bottom line

For the current 2020 iPad Pro, keep searching for a used/open-box genuine Apple Pencil 2 if pressure in ZBrush matters. SonarPen 2 and Adonit Note+ are genuine pressure-sensitive budget pens, but their pressure support is app-specific and was not confirmed for ZBrush.

For the new setup, compare Apple Education pricing against Best Buy open-box and trade-in offers on the actual purchase day. The 13-inch iPad Pro is the preferred creative workstation size; choose storage/RAM based on how much high-poly ZBrush work will live on the tablet versus the desktop Unreal workstation.
