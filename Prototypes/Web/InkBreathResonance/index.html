✦ Melodia Melusina

A rhythm-JRPG where music is the key. Play instruments to unlock routes, harmonize with the world, and chart the resonance of dreams.

The First Dream chapter establishes the core loop: narrative initiation, overworld traversal, rhythm combat, resolution, traversal upgrade, checkpoint. Melusina awakens in the Sanctuary and must harmonize with the Sea Above to unlock her first instrument — the Sonic Scepter.

Twelve instruments map to the chromatic scale. Each unlocks traversal capabilities: the Sonic Scepter reveals hidden paths, the Phase Bell bends local time, the Crescendo Fan releases waves from soft to cataclysmic.
